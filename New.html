Welcome to Rugby World

Rugby is a popular sport played by millions of people around the world. It is known for its physical strength, teamwork and exciting gameplay.

The game is played between two teams who try to score points by carrying or kicking the ball into the opponent’s goal area.

Rugby requires discipline, fitness and strategy. Players must work together to move the ball and defend their territory.

The sport originated in England and has grown into an international game played in many countries.

Major competitions like the Rugby World Cup attract fans from all over the world.

Basic Rugby Skills

  1. Passing
  2. Tackling
  3. Kicking
  4. Running
  5. Scrummaging

Benefits of Playing Rugby

  • Improves fitness
  • Builds teamwork
  • Develops discipline
  • Improves strength
